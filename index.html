About Learnive

Making Queue Management Easier

Learnive is a Virtual Queue Management System designed to make student service management simple, fast and organised.

Students can generate tokens online, track their queue status and know when their turn arrives without waiting in long physical queues.

Administrators can manage customers, call the next customer, update service status and monitor queue activities through a single dashboard.

Our Key Features

Easy Token Generation

Generate tokens online easily.

Live Queue Tracking

Check your token status anytime.

Admin Dashboard

Manage and monitor customer queues.

Queue Analytics

View service-wise customer statistics.

How It Works

Step 1: Join the Queue

Enter your name and select the required service.

Step 2: Get Your Token

Receive a unique token number automatically.

Step 3: Track Your Status

Enter your token number to check your position.

Step 4: Get Your Service

Visit the service counter when your turn arrives.
